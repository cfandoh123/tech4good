Your group projects.

Read your project description, work through the logic flow step by step, and build it with the stuff we have already learned: variables, if / else, and printing the answer back out.

Group 01

Density Calculator

Your app finds whatever value is missing (mass, volume, or density) using one formula. Rearrange it depending on what the user wants to find.

The formula
Density = Mass ÷ Volume measured in g/cm³ Mass    = Density × Volume measured in g Volume  = Mass ÷ Density measured in cm³
Logic flow
  1. Ask the user: “What do you want to find: Mass, Volume, or Density?”
  2. Ask for the two values you still need, based on their answer.
  3. Plug the numbers into the matching formula above.
  4. Display the result with the correct unit attached.
Group 02

ECG Prepaid Biller

Your app estimates a monthly electricity bill from the appliances someone uses and how long they run them each day.

The formula
Step 1 Energy (kWh) = (Wattage ÷ 1000) × Hours used per day × 30 days Step 2 Cost (GH₵) = Total Energy (kWh) × Cost per kWh
Logic flow
  1. Show the user the appliance list below and ask which ones they use.
  2. For each appliance they select, ask how many hours a day they use it.
  3. Calculate the energy used by each appliance, then add them all together.
  4. Multiply the total by the cost per kWh to get the estimated bill.
  5. Tell the user if their bill is Low, Moderate, or High, using the thresholds below.

Group 03

BMI and Health Teller

Your app calculates Body Mass Index (BMI) from weight and height, then sorts the result into a category.

The formula
BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ [ Height (m) × Height (m) ]
Logic flow
  1. Ask the user for their weight in kilograms.
  2. Ask the user for their height in metres
  3. Calculate BMI using the formula above.
  4. Compare the result to the categories below and display the matching one.
BMI categories
